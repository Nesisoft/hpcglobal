🗓

07

Give / Donate

The online offering plate. Must be simple, trustworthy, and mobile-optimised. Mobile Money is the primary payment method for most Ghanaian givers.

hpcglobal.org /give
Page Introduction
🙏
Giving call to action — spiritual framing
Short statement: why giving matters, grounded in scripture (Malachi 3:10, 2 Cor 9:7, etc.). Not transactional — it should feel like an act of worship. Quote from Prophet Clottey on giving if available.
Required
Giving Form

Form fields

  • Amount (quick-select: GH₵20 / 50 / 100 / 200 / 500 + custom)
  • Giving category: Tithes / Offering / First Fruits / Building Fund / Missions / Pastoral Support
  • Payment method selector
  • Full name
  • Phone number (for MoMo prompt)
  • Email (optional, for receipt)

Payment methods

  • MTN Mobile Money (MoMo API or manual number)
  • Telecel Cash
  • AirtelTigo Money
  • Bank transfer (account details shown)
  • Card payment (Paystack / Flutterwave — Phase 2)
  • International: PayPal / Wise (Phase 2, for diaspora)
Diaspora giving: Since HPC Global has an international following (EST / BST timezones), consider adding PayPal or Wise for overseas members in Phase 2.
Giving Categories Explained
📖
Category descriptions
Short 1-sentence explanation of each giving category so members understand what their money supports. Builds trust and encourages larger or more specific giving.
High value
Recurring Giving
🔁
Monthly giving option
Allow members to set up a monthly standing amount — especially useful for tithers. Can be managed manually (via MoMo standing instructions) or via Paystack subscription in Phase 2.

New Here?

The most important conversion page. A first-time visitor who lands here should leave feeling warmly welcomed, confident about visiting, and clear on their next step.

hpcglobal.org /new-here
Welcome Message
👋
Personal welcome from Prophet & Lady Apostle Clottey
A short, warm, personal video or written welcome message from the senior pastors. This humanises the church instantly. Even a 60-second video is transformative for first-time visitors. If video isn't available, a signed letter-style message with their photo.
Required
What to Expect

In-person visit

  • Arrival: what happens when you walk in
  • Worship style: expectant, Spirit-led
  • The Word: prophetic, practical preaching
  • Altar call / ministry time
  • Dress: smart casual, come as you are
  • Duration: ~2.5 hours (Dominion Encounter)
  • Parking / getting there
  • Children: where they go

Online / first visit

  • How to join YouTube live stream
  • How to join Prophetic Highway on Zoom
  • How to participate in the chat
  • How to receive prayer online
  • How to give online
  • How to connect after the service
New Visitor Form
📝
Connect card (digital)
Name, phone, email, location (country/city for international), how they found HPC Global, and "What are you hoping to find?" (optional). On submission: automated WhatsApp or SMS welcome from the church, and a follow-up call from the welcome team within 48 hours.
Required
Next Steps Guide
→
Journey steps (3-step visual)
Step 1: Visit a service. Step 2: Connect with a ministry. Step 3: Get planted — join a cell group and become a member. Simple, clear, and aspirational.
