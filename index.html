Our story

Raising a generation of kingdom champions

Hopepress Chapel is an Apostolic Prophetic Word-based ministry which brings hope to the hopeless through the preaching of the Word of Hope — welcoming every soul into a place of acceptance.

Our Vision

To bring hope to the hopeless by the preaching of the Word of Hope, and bring them to a place of acceptance.

Our Mission

To accept the rejected and the frustrated, and raise them as Kingdom leaders through the teaching and preaching of the Word of Hope — birthing them into their original place of influence.

"For I know the plans I have for you, declares the Lord — plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you a hope and a future."
Jeremiah 29:11
